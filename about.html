About us

Where the road becomes a community.

Rally2Rumble is a regional car rally for drivers, passengers and partners who share one passion: a full day on the road, done properly.

01 Our story

Made for people who love the drive.

Rally2Rumble is a community of drivers, passengers and partners who believe the best days are spent on the road together. We combine a carefully planned route with a relaxed, premium atmosphere, so that every team can enjoy the day from check-in to prize giving.

02

What we do

The rally

One route, one set of stages, one day on the road. Every team follows the same turn-by-turn directions, from the start to the finish.

The community

A place where teams, drivers and guests meet before and after the day, share photos and stay in touch.

The partners

Local businesses join us to reach an engaged audience of car enthusiasts, with visibility on the cars, in the paddock and online.

03

Mission and vision

Our mission

To bring drivers, passengers and partners together for a well-organised day on the road that people want to come back to.

Our vision

A respected regional rally and a lasting community of car enthusiasts and local businesses that grows with every edition.
